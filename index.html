01 — A short introduction

Software that actually works
under real load.

I've spent the last four years writing software that real people depend on — from telecom systems handling mobile-number lifecycles for one of South Asia's largest operators, to a US enterprise document-signing workflow, to my current work on a hospital management platform that clinicians use every day.

My natural home is the backend: Java, Spring Boot, REST APIs, microservices, PL/SQL, and the infrastructure that keeps it all running — Docker, Kubernetes, Jenkins. But I'm just as comfortable on the other side, building responsive React interfaces in TypeScript when a project needs an engineer who can own a feature end to end.

I care about clean code, strong tests, and clear thinking. I'd rather ship one well-designed service than five clever-but-fragile ones. Outside of work, I'm a published author at ICSOAC-23 for an AI-based crop recommendation system, and I'm currently learning German.
